Session 3: 6. Read the data like a driver

Example only: distFromStart 500, speedX 80, accel 0 and brake 0.2 means “500 metres around the track, we are going 80 km/h, easing off the accelerator and applying a light brake command”, assuming standard SCR units. A short forward sensor means the edge is close along that ray; it may reflect a corner, car heading or position. It is not automatically the nearest wall or a crash.

trackPos describes sideways position; angle describes heading relative to the track. min_valid_track is the shortest valid sensor ray, not a precise sideways clearance. Damage increases are stronger evidence of contact than a distance reading alone.

Session 3: 9. Understand the corner sequence

LOOK AHEAD: recognise a possible bend. CHOOSE A SPEED: select a cautious target for that road section. SLOW BEFORE TURN-IN: ease off or brake when the controller logic calls for it. TURN SMOOTHLY: avoid sudden corrections. EXIT: gradually accelerate when the road opens and the car is stable.

A dead-band is a small margin before braking; a target of 80 with a margin of 5 would mean considering braking above 85. This is a teaching example, not a recommended setting. Do not brake and accelerate against each other unless the controller explicitly has a justified policy.

Setup appendix: Common connection steps — all platforms

1. Launch the VERIFIED challenge-compatible simulator. 2. Choose the required race, track and car. Select the external SCR/server-controlled driver; a keyboard or built-in AI driver will not test Python. Exact menu labels depend on the package. 3. Inspect the script’s host and UDP port and match the server configuration. 127.0.0.1/localhost and UDP 3001 are common defaults, not verified settings for every package. 4. Start the configured race until ready for the external client, then run the baseline in a second terminal. Follow the package if its startup order differs. 5. Confirm incoming readings AND the correct car moving under the controller. “Connecting” alone is not success. 6. Let a full timed lap complete, record damage and stop the client before edits. 7. Only one client should control the assigned car/port. Keep baseline and experiment conditions consistent.

Do not copy wrapper flags such as -nodamage or -nolaptime into this challenge: they can defeat damage or timing evaluation. Network tuning cannot repair a controller that is connected but steering badly.
